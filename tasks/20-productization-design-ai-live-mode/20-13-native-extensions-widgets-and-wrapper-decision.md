# 20-13 — Native Extensions, Widgets And Wrapper Decision

**Estimate:** 1 hour

## Goal

Decide whether mobile widgets and deeper native platform features belong in a
PWA-only roadmap or require a native wrapper/app.

## Scope

- iOS widget feasibility.
- Android widget feasibility.
- PWA limitations.
- Native wrapper options.
- What remains web/PWA MVP.
- What is explicitly deferred.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Widgets do not silently disappear from the roadmap.
- Platform limitations are explicit.
- The decision is recorded as implement now, defer, or reject.
- Any native-wrapper work becomes a separate future phase.
